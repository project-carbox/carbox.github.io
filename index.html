SCIENTIFIC VISION

The Fundamental Crystallographic Challenge

Why atomic distance alone is deceptive without rigorous electron density and thermal displacement validation.

The False Positive Hazard

In standard crystallographic refinement, side-chain carboxylates (Asp, Glu) in highly flexible loops can be artificially fitted into ambiguous, low-density features. A measured distance of d ≤ 2.50 Å often represents a modeling artifact or unmodeled metal bridge rather than a true hydrogen bond.

"If there is no electron density for atoms, they are mismodeled. If atoms are mismodeled, the distance between them is completely meaningless." — Prof. Garib Murshudov (MRC-LMB Cambridge)

The Carbox 4-Stage Precision Solution

Rather than blindly accepting PDB coordinates, Carbox applies a multi-tier decision tree combining Tukey Interquartile Range (IQR) outlier detection on isotropic B-factors, Fisher z-transformed real-space correlation coefficients (RSCC), empirical PROPKA pKa shift evaluation, and 70-metal coordination screening.

  • Eliminates thermal disorder noise (α = 1.5 & 3.0 Tukey whiskers)
  • Discards unmodeled Zn²⁺, Mg²⁺, Ca²⁺ deprotonated salts
  • Confirms genuine protonation shifts (ΔpKa ≥ +2.0)
METHODOLOGY

Prof. Garib Murshudov's 4-Stage Protocol

From raw high-resolution diffraction coordinates to verified biochemical protonation states.

STAGE 1

Euclidean Contact Mining & Solvent Burial

All atomic-resolution macromolecular crystal structures (≤ 1.20 Å) are indexed. The algorithm extracts side-chain carboxyl oxygens (Asp OD1/OD2, Glu OE1/OE2) and computes all inter-residue Euclidean pairwise distances:

$$d(O_i, O_j) = \sqrt{(x_i - x_j)^2 + (y_i - y_j)^2 + (z_i - z_j)^2} \le 3.20\,\text{Å}$$

The centroid of each pair is computed and cross-referenced with all crystallographic water molecules. Pairs with d(water) > 3.0 Å are tagged as Buried (ε ≈ 2–4, high dielectric penalty favoring protonation).

Stage 1 Screening Thresholds

  • Crystallographic Resolution≤ 1.20 Å (Tier 1)
  • Carboxyl Contact Cutoffd ≤ 3.20 Å
  • Ultra-Short LBHB Cutoffd ≤ 2.55 Å
  • Solvent Exclusion Cutoffd(water) > 3.00 Å
STAGE 2

Crystallographic Precision & Noise Pruning

As instructed by Prof. Garib Murshudov, pairs are subjected to a rigorous dual-prong validation protocol:

A. Non-Parametric Tukey IQR Filter
$$B_{\text{cutoff}} = Q_3 + \alpha \cdot \text{IQR} \quad (\text{IQR} = Q_3 - Q_1, \; \alpha \in \{1.5, 3.0\})$$

If either oxygen has $B > B_{\text{cutoff}}$ or $B_z = (B - \mu_B)/\sigma_B > 3.0$, the contact is classified as thermal disorder noise and rejected.

B. Real-Space Correlation with Fisher Z-Transform
$$z = \frac{1}{2} \ln \left(\frac{1 + \text{CC}'}{1 - \text{CC}'}\right), \quad \text{CC}' = \min(0.9999, \text{CC})$$

Atoms with standardized $Z_{\text{CC}} < -3.0$ exhibit poor density agreement and are pruned.

STAGE 3

Biochemical Protonation Modeling (PROPKA)

Isolated carboxylates in neutral water have standard pKa values of 3.8 (Asp) and 4.5 (Glu). Bringing two negative carboxylates to within 2.50 Å requires a tremendous electrostatic penalty that is only stabilized by a shared bridging proton.

$$\Delta \text{p}K_a = \text{p}K_a^{\text{local}} - \text{p}K_a^{\text{model}} \ge +2.0 \quad \Longrightarrow \quad \text{p}K_a \ge 6.0\text{--}8.0$$

Using PROPKA 3 integration, Carbox verifies that candidate LBHB carboxyl pairs exhibit dramatic upward pKa shifts, confirming that one residue functions as a neutral hydrogen donor at physiological pH.

Protonation Landscape

  • Aqueous Asp pKa3.80
  • Aqueous Glu pKa4.50
  • Buried LBHB Asp pKa6.50 – 8.20
  • Protonation ConfidenceStrong (>95%)
STAGE 4

10–20 Golden Benchmarks & Literature Validation

Before global batch scaling, the methodology is verified manually against 10–20 universally celebrated, experimentally proven catalytic and structural LBHBs:

  • 1KZK: HIV-1 Retroviral Protease catalytic dyad (Asp25···Asp25')
  • 1OEW: Endothiapepsin sub-angstrom aspartic dyad (0.90 Å)
  • 1FKN: BACE-1 Alzheimer beta-secretase dyad (Asp32···Asp228)
  • 2DSX: Rubredoxin sub-atomic buried core (0.68 Å, Asp35···Asp47)
  • 4ZM7: Aminopeptidase proton-relay network (0.70 Å, Asp149···Glu162)
